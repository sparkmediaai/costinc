Our Work

Zoos, aquariums, museums, theme parks and more.

Six decades from its inception, COST’s award-winning work can be visited at zoos, theme parks, casinos, resorts, museums, botanical gardens, golf courses and restaurants throughout the world.

01 Zoological Zoo habitat fabrication is one of our specialties and is where we got our start. 02 Aquariums Our work in public and private freshwater and saltwater aquariums spans the globe. 03 Museums Experiential displays that will inspire, delight, and educate. 04 Theme Parks Landscape elements that create the terrain and set the stage for memorable experiences. 05 Casinos Central interior and exterior focal points in popular casinos. 06 Commercial Specialty theme construction that helps your business stand out from the crowd. 07 Water Features Fountains, reflecting pools and waterfalls that make a huge impact on your space. 08 Resorts Themes, water features and artistic features for any hotel or resort property. 09 Sculptures Any type of feature or sculpture, real or imagined. 10 Theme Signage Signs developed by COST are 100% custom fabricated.
