Our Work

Sculpture Fabrication

Any type of feature or sculpture, real or imagined.

Sculptures have always been important elements in many theme parks, family entertainment complexes, museums, zoological parks, and even festival grounds.

Since 1957, COST’s skilled artisans have specialized in developing sculptures and scenery for all types of entertainment and educational venues. Creativity, attention to detail, and expertise in many fabrication methodologies enable us to deliver any type of feature or sculpture, real or imagined.

Sculpture Fabrication Capabilities

Our staffed sculptors have mastered a wide variety of mediums and techniques to create authentic reproductions of people, animals, creatures, facades, sets, objects, and more. We also offer a state-of-the-art 5-Axis CNC machine which quickly and accurately converts 3D drawings or physical models into a scaled feature.

Whether your project requires a detailed replica or a fantasy-inspired, whimsical sculpture, COST will work closely with your team to deliver the perfect sculptural piece within any realistic budget.
