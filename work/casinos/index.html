Our Work

Casino Theme Construction

Central interior and exterior focal points in popular casinos.

The casino industry has evolved from gambling facilities to exciting entertainment destinations. Many casino themes transport guests to different places around the world, creating a sense of wonder.

They feel like an exotic escape and offer shopping, eating, and more, interspersed with slot machines and table games.

COST’s Expertise in Casino Theme Design & Fabrication

COST has helped to create many of the central interior and exterior focal points in popular casinos, as well as the surrounding themes. Successful casino design begins with a spark of imagination.

Often, our specialty features are placed in highly visible areas of the casino, such as porte-cochères, lobbies, food hall areas, restaurants, lounges, gift shops, and of course, the gaming floor. The design of these spaces must be well thought out. Casino themes set the tone and help to differentiate one gaming facility from another in this competitive industry.

Designing a unique and remarkable experience requires creativity and collaboration on the team. Our artisans work side-by-side with casino architects, interior designers, general contractors, and casino owners to ensure we have final approval and bring their ideas into reality.

What begins with working drawings comes to life with physical features and artistic construction in the casino environment. Casino theme construction is an endeavor requiring many diverse mediums. Our personnel use theme carpentry, GFRC, FRP, GFRG, acrylics, epoxies, decorative metals, glass, and more. We apply design principles, such as adding natural light and décor elements that complement the environment and building design.

Our design team is well-versed in an array of fabrication techniques, materials, and construction disciplines. We offer a single-source solution for casino themes and related specialty construction projects.

Trust Experienced Casino Theme Construction Experts

From 1993 to today, our firm has assisted with the casino theme construction on over 50 tribal projects from Southern California to Northern Washington, Florida to Connecticut.

Not only has the COST development team worked on prestigious Las Vegas projects like Caesar’s Palace, The Wynn, and many more, but we’ve also worked extensively with Native American gaming. As an active member of the National Indian Gaming Association (opens in a new tab) (NIGA), Washington Indian Gaming Association (opens in a new tab) (WIGA), and American Indian Chamber of Commerce of Wisconsin (opens in a new tab) (AICCW), we recognize that many tribal properties are more than just casinos.

Our expert casino theme designers work closely with architects to integrate unique tribal heritage into the designs, many of which include a convention center, event space, or exhibition area. As with all COST projects, the success of our casino theme results from collaboration between owners, developers, designers, and construction teams.

If you’re considering a casino theme project, COST is ready to assist. For more information on creating a fantastic guest experience, please contact us today!
