Our Work

Aquarium Exhibit Fabrication

Our work in public and private freshwater and saltwater aquariums spans the globe.

COST has been actively involved in aquatic habitat fabrication since 1957. We have been proud members of the AZA (Association of Zoos and Aquariums) (opens in a new tab) for over 40 years.

Our work in public and private freshwater and saltwater aquariums spans the globe. Underwater exhibits vary by region, from river basins to brackish waters to ocean coral reefs. COST site personnel have extensive knowledge in creating immersive aquarium creations and many other specialty aquatic environments. Materials used in these aquarium exhibits are animal-safe, durable, fade-resistant, and highly realistic.

Trust COST for Beautiful Water Exhibits

We understand the care and precision required to create lifelike exhibits. Underwater exhibits may vary by region, exhibit type, and more. We can create realistic river basins, interactive-friendly brackish waters, stunning ocean coral reefs, and any other aquatic environment in between.

One of the biggest challenges in any water-based exhibition is that the materials must withstand exposure to aquatic conditions. When animal life is included in an exhibit, installation, or aquarium, there’s another layer to the challenge. Materials in aquarium habitats must be animal-safe and durable. We work with aquarium staff to conduct extensive research, ensuring that public aquariums always put health and safety first.

As with all our faux rockwork and exhibit installations, all materials must be durable, fade-resistant, and highly realistic. Many live animal exhibits are part of education programs and conservation efforts. Visitors should feel as though every part of an exhibit is authentic. We can help you create those memorable, imaginative-themed environments that inspire and inform.

Simulated Coral for Realistic Aquatic Environments

The stunning beauty of coral is one of nature’s true wonders. Our creative artisans have worked to perfect simulated coral so it’s undetectable from any coral you’d find in nature.

Simulated coral (opens in a new tab) is available through our abundant cast library and as sculptural elements through custom aquarium fabrication. Our current library of Atlantic, Pacific, Indian Ocean, and Red Sea coral casts (opens in a new tab) provides specific authenticity to any underwater environment. It includes true-to-life corals, staghorns, sea plums, whips, stars, and grasses.

Coral creates a beautiful, welcoming habitat for underwater creatures, adds visual interest, and is integral to any aquatic landscape. Our knowledge of coral placement and configuration ensures that any reef project will rival that of natural reefs found around the world.
